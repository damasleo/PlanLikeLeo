About PlanLikeLeo

PlanLikeLeo is your all-in-one digital planner designed for students who want to take control of their academic journey. Our mission is to empower you to achieve your goals, reduce stress, and enjoy a more organized, productive, and fulfilling student life.

Whether you’re tracking assignments, planning your week, or jotting down ideas, PlanLikeLeo is here to help you stay on top of everything. Built by students, for students.
